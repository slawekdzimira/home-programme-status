Defect report · 29 Sep 2026

Tenant AR demo against the real DMS flow

The hidden demo at tenant-ar-demo.html is meant to show the DMS tenant annual recertification one to one. It does not yet: 4 defects break it in front of a viewer or lose their progress, and most screens differ from DMS in steps, copy, components, loaders and images. 44 findings below, each with where it happens in the demo, what DMS does, the fix and a way to reproduce.

Loaders and saving feedback

AR-01 Major

No loader anywhere: a blank white page on open, and every step jumps without feedback

every route · tenant-ar-demo.html (empty <div data-tenant-root>), tenant.js:1658-1667

In the demo

Nothing is painted until persona.json has loaded: the page is plain white for about 1.0 s on desktop and 2 s on a phone on a Slow 4G profile (measured, frames below). After that, every button swaps the screen instantly, so answers and saves give no sign that anything happened.

In DMS

LoadingView opens on page load and on every save: blurred backdrop, the 94 px modal banner strip, a 65 px turquoise circle with a purple cog turning once every 4 s, the title “Please take a moment of patience” with animated trailing dots, and the body “We are downloading the latest data” (loading) or “We are processing your request” (saving). It is a native <dialog>, so focus is trapped. Buttons never show their own spinner; they are only disabled.

Source: src/components/loading-view/LoadingView.tsx:33-53, src/styles/animations.css:1-50, en-us.json:15-17; seen at 0:28, 0:36 and 11:40 of the recording

Fix

Put the purple solid-layout ground and a static copy of the LoadingView card straight into tenant-ar-demo.html, so the first paint is never white, and remove it once demo.init() resolves. Show the same card with the “processing your request” body for 600 to 900 ms on every action that saves in DMS: Yes/No answers, Save, Confirm, Sign, Submit.

Reproduce

Documents, uploads and photos

AR-04 Blocker

Every sample document image is broken

all Review & Confirm Information screens · assets/pha-ar-demo/docs/{id,ssc,birth-cert,ssi,bank,medicare}.svg, lines 4 and 11

In the demo

All six sample SVGs contain the control byte 0x14 (a mangled dash) in the banner text on line 4 (CARD \x14 DEMO) and an invalid byte in the footer on line 11. XML does not allow that byte, so the browser refuses the file and shows a broken-image icon with the alt text (“Social Security card preview”). Checked: naturalWidth is 0 for all six, and an XML parser reports “not well-formed (invalid token): line 4”.

In DMS

DMS shows no image here at all (see AR-06).

Fix

Replace the byte with a plain hyphen, save the files as UTF-8 and add xmllint --noout assets/pha-ar-demo/docs/*.svg to the build. The files are still needed as the thing the file-name link opens (AR-10).

Reproduce
  1. Open the demo, then Menu (☰) > Reset demo for a clean household (the ?fresh=1 link does not reset, see AR-19).
  2. Pencil on Maya Solis > Social Security card > Use demo packet.
  3. Wait for the review screen: the top of the card shows a broken image.
AR-05 Blocker

One real phone photo silently stops all saving, and a reload loses the progress

any upload · store.js:133-139 (persist()), store.js:368-373, tenant.js:326-337

In the demo

Uploads are stored as data: URLs inside the one localStorage entry. A 5.6 MB phone JPEG becomes 7.4 million characters, setItem throws QuotaExceededError, and persist() ignores it. From then on nothing is saved. In the test, after the photo the Social Security card and the birth certificate of the new member were confirmed; a reload brought the viewer back to the ID upload with both confirmations gone, and the Summary then listed 3 issues.

In DMS

Files are uploaded to the server; nothing is lost on reload.

Fix

Never put file bytes in localStorage. Keep the File in memory or in IndexedDB keyed by slot id and store only {name, size, type} in the state. If any save fails, show an error toast instead of ignoring it.

Reproduce
  1. Open the demo, then Menu (☰) > Reset demo for a clean household (the ?fresh=1 link does not reset, see AR-19).
  2. Menu > Family Members > Add new member + > Continue.
  3. Choose a real phone photo (3 MB or more) and Continue through two documents.
  4. Reload the page.
