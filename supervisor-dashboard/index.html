Decisions for HOME

Three choices shape the final layout.

The six headline figures

Confirm the top row, or name the figures HOME would rather see there. Any figure the dashboard computes today can take a place.

A target for each

The green and red marks compare each figure with a target. The administrator records targets under Metric Thresholds; none is recorded for El Paso yet.

Who opens this view

Supervisors only, or leadership as well. A leadership view can open on the headline figures and highlights, without the queue strip.

Not in this proposal: peer and national benchmarks, resident satisfaction and SEMAP standing. Each needs figures the supervisor dashboard does not receive today.
